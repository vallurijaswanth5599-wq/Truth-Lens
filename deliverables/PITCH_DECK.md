# TruthLens — Hackathon Pitch Deck (10 Slides)

## Slide 1: Title
- **TruthLens**: Empirical AI Misinformation Verification Platform
- *Subtitle*: Verify News, Text, URLs, Images, Videos, and Documents Before You Share
- **Team Nova** | AI & Cybersecurity Track

## Slide 2: The Problem
- **The Information Pandemic**: Over 60% of viral social media posts contain misleading or synthetic AI content.
- **Deepfake Proliferation**: AI-generated media is generated in seconds, bypassing conventional human fact-checking teams.
- **Consequences**: Harm to public health, financial market fraud, election distortion, and brand risk.

## Slide 3: The Solution
- **TruthLens Platform**: An automated, multi-modality RAG verification engine.
- **Zero Hallucination Guarantee**: Grounded strictly in live Tavily deep web retrieval & 220+ domain trust scoring database.
- **Multi-Vector Forensics**: C2PA digital watermarks, EXIF metadata, Shannon byte entropy, and latent diffusion noise analysis.

## Slide 4: AI Architecture
- **Stage 1**: Claim Extraction & Query Expansion
- **Stage 2**: Live Tavily Search & 220+ Categorized Domain Trust Engine
- **Stage 3**: Gemini 2.5 Flash Chain-of-Thought (CoT) Reasoning Protocol
- **Stage 4**: Multi-Vector Media & URL Reputation Forensics
- **Stage 5**: Dynamic Multi-Factor Weighted Scoring

## Slide 5: Key Features
- **6 Supported Input Modalities**: Text, URL, Image, Video, Document, Screenshot.
- **Calibrated Verdicts**: `Safe to Share`, `Share with Caution`, `Do Not Share`, `Requires Expert Review`, `Insufficient Evidence`.
- **Explainable Report**: Interactive claim-by-claim breakdown with direct click-through source citations.

## Slide 6: Tech Stack
- **Frontend**: React 19, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Framer Motion, Axios
- **Backend**: Python FastAPI, SQLAlchemy ORM, JWT Auth, Pydantic v2
- **Databases & Cache**: PostgreSQL (Neon), Redis
- **AI Models**: Google Gemini 2.5 Flash, Tavily Deep Search API

## Slide 7: Live Product Demo
- *Live Demonstration of Text Claim Verification & AI Image Deepfake Detection.*

## Slide 8: Business Model
- **B2C Premium Subscription**: Unlimited verifications, browser extensions, PDF export.
- **B2B API & Enterprise License**: Social media platform API integrations, newsroom fact-checking plugins, enterprise threat intelligence.

## Slide 9: Competitive Advantage
- **RAG-Grounded vs Plain LLMs**: Conventional ChatGPT/Claude hallucinate facts; TruthLens strictly enforces web evidence grounding.
- **Dual-Engine Image Inspection**: Combines physical binary buffer forensics with visual neural models.

## Slide 10: Future Roadmap
- Browser extension for real-time social media feed auditing.
- Multi-lingual audio voice cloning verification engine.
- On-chain C2PA provenance ledger recording.
